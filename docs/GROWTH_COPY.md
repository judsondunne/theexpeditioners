## Library or classroom resource introduction

For readers who like a puzzle with their adventure: The Expeditioners site has a free book-cipher activity with a worked example and interactive encoder, plus a printable mapmaker’s field sheet and discussion questions for all three books.

Book One, *The Treasure of Drowned Man’s Canyon*, was selected for the Texas Bluebonnet Award Master List (2014–15) and Dorothy Canfield Fisher Award Master List (2013–14). Kirkus recommends ages 10–14; School Library Journal recommends grades 4–7.
