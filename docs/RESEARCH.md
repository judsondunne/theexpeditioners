## Premise and canon (VERIFIED unless noted)

- Computers invented in **1880**, failed **a century later**; strange lands not on any map; steam and clockwork replace gasoline and electricity (PW, Kids VT, Boing Boing).
- Siblings: **Kit** (13, narrator, "the brain"), **Zander** (14, "the brave"), **M.K.** (youngest, inventor, "the tinkerer") — PW, Kirkus, McSweeney's jacket copy / author site.
- **Alexander West**, famed Explorer of the Realm, presumed dead in the **Fazian rainforest**; the **Explorer with the Clockwork Hand** leads Kit to half of a strange map (Book 1 back cover; Kirkus).
- **Bureau of Newly Discovered Lands** (corrupt; covets the treasure — PW). "Bureau of Land and Sea Exploration" does not exist.
- **Academy for the Exploratory Sciences**; hidden Caribbean island known for deadly storms (Book 2 back cover); mechanical sea turtles and amphibious killer eels (Kirkus Book 2).
- **Simerian Desert**; a secret desert city known only in legend (Book 3 back cover).
- Katherine Roy's jacket map labels: Drowned Man's Canyon, Azure Canyon/Creek/City/Falls/Peak, Foley's Peak, Twin Peaks, Phantom Peak, Ghost Falls.
- Other names found in reviews (Sukey Neville, Pucci the parrot, Leo/Lazlo Nackley, Mr. Mountmorris, Hilda Magnusdottir) are **not yet used** on the site.

## People

- **S. S. Taylor = Sarah Stewart Taylor** — VERIFIED (her `/expeditioners` page, Hachette, Roy's blog, PW). Lives on a Vermont farm (sheep, blueberries); former journalist and teacher; adult mysteries (Sweeney St. George, Maggie D'arcy, *Agony Hill*, *Hunter's Heart Ridge*, Minotaur); graphic novel *Amelia Earhart: This Broad Ocean* (with Ben Towle). Loved maps since age ten after finding a mistake on a neighbourhood map (old series site — author's own bio).
- **Katherine Roy** — katherineroy.com; *Neighborhood Sharks* (Sibert Honor), *How to Be an Elephant*, *Making More*; western Oregon. Illustrated Books 1–2 (VERIFIED); Book 3 credited only by retailers (**confirm**).
